Kevin Kappelmann (Sep 15 2026 at 20:09):

Hi Bob, below paper by Kuncar and Popescu deals with the consistency of Isabelle/HOL's axioms.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10817-018-9454-8

Function application is just beta reduction, which is one of the axioms of Isabelle.
The type checker is part of the kernel.
The proof system is part of the kernel.
Type and term instantiation are Isabelle axioms.
