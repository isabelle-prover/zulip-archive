Adem Rimpapa (Apr 08 2025 at 15:13):

I am trying to build several sessions, some of which have the option document=pdf enabled in their ROOT files. I want to build the sessions without generating the documents, but running something like
isabelle build ... -o document=false does not seem to work. Is this not the right way to suppress the document generation for this specific build?

Bob Rubbens (Nov 11 2026 at 09:53):

Sorry to necro this thread. Did you ever figure it out? I'm running into the same issue.

Kevin Kappelmann (Nov 11 2026 at 10:10):

Session options take precedence, so you cannot overwrite it like that.
If the session does not set document_variants, you may be able to skip document generation by using -o document_variants= (i.e. empty document variants)
