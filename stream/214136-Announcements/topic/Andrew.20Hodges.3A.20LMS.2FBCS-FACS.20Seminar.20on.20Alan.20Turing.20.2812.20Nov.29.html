Topic: Andrew Hodges: LMS/BCS-FACS Seminar on Alan Turing (12 Nov)

Andrei Popescu (Sep 25 2026 at 23:29):

We are delighted to announce that Andrew Hodges (University of Oxford) will give this year's LMS/BCS-FACS Seminar on 12 November 2026, with a talk titled "Alan Turing, The Absent Father of Computing".

The seminar also marks an anniversary: Turing's "On Computable Numbers" paper was read to the London Mathematical Society on 12 November 1936, ninety years to the day!

The event will be held in a hybrid format: in person at De Morgan House in London or online via Zoom.

Date: Thursday, 12 November 2026
Time: 16:30-17:30 GMT

Attendance is free, but advance registration is required. Full details, abstract and registration:
https://www.lms.ac.uk/events/lms-bcs-facs-seminar-2026
