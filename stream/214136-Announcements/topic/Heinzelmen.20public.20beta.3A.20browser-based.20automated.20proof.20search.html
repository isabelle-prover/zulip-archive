Topic: Heinzelmen public beta: browser-based automated proof search

Yutaka Nagashima (Aug 16 2026 at 15:26):

I have finally launched Heinzelmen, a browser-based automated theorem-proving service for Isabelle/HOL.

:www: https://heinzelmen.org

  • No installation.
  • No setup.
  • No registration.
  • No subscription.
  • No API fees.

Just you and a .thy file with exactly one sorry.

Due to the severely limited computational resources currently available to us, the public beta comes with several restrictions:

  • one Isabelle/HOL .thy file;
  • fewer than 500 LoC;
  • exactly one sorry, which is replaced with a proof if search succeeds;
  • Main only;
  • no neural booster for proof search.

For this reason, I do not expect the current service to be particularly useful to professional or experienced Isabelle users, like many of you here. Rather, I hope it will be useful to Isabelle beginners and curious students who want to experiment with automated proof search without setting up a local environment.

We are also very interested in collaboration, for example, hosting other automated proof-search engines, exploring support for other proof assistants, or improving the computational resources available to the service.

Feedback, collaboration, and support are very welcome.

Enjoy!
