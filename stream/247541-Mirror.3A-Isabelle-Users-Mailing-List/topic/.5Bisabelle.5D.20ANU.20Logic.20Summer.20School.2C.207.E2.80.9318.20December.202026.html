34th Annual ANU Logic Summer School
December 7 – 18, 2026
Canberra, Ngunnawal and Ngambri Country, Australia
comp.anu.edu.au/lss<http://comp.anu.edu.au/lss>

OVERVIEW

The ANU Logic Summer School is an annual event that offers a two week long programme of lectures on modern logic, the foundational discipline of the information sciences. Topics include not only the science of reasoning but also computability theory, type theory and other tools for understanding processes, declarative programming, automatic proof generation, program verification and much more. The school is primarily geared at late undergraduate and masters students, but is open to all, including postgraduate and PhD students, postdocs, and participants from industry.

PROGRAMME

The school features four introductory courses in the first week, and a series of five lectures covering more advanced topics in the second week. The courses are given by a mix of local, domestic, and international speakers.

Week 1

* John Slaney (ANU): Fundamentals of Metalogic
* Ranald Clouston (ANU): Foundations (Plural) of Mathematics
* Peter Höfner (ANU): Introduction to CTL Model Checking
* Liam O’Connor (ANU): Introduction to Interactive Theorem-Proving with Isabelle

Week 2

* Amal Ahmed (Northeastern University) : Logical Relations: From Type Soundness to Compiler Correctness and Specification of ABIs
* Natasha Dobrinen (University of Notre Dame): Infinite Ramsey Theory and Logic
* Rod Downey (Victoria University of Wellington): Algorithmic Information Theory
* K. Rustan M. Leino (Harmonic) : Proofs as Part of Programming
* Sara Negri (University of Genoa) : Proof Theory of Modal and Non-Classical Logics: the Method of Labelled Sequent Calculi

SOCIAL EVENTS

The summer school hosts a welcome reception on Sunday, 6 December, a social dinner on Tuesday, 15 December, and organises an excursion to Canberra’s surrounds on Saturday, 12 December.

ACCOMMODATION

We do not organise accommodation for participants. The Canberra Accommodation Centre<http://www.canberra-accommodation.com.au/> is reasonably priced and in walking distance from the ANU campus.

REGISTRATION

The registration fee for students is A$160 and includes the social events. Details on how to register, and fees for other categories of participants, are on the school’s website<http://comp.anu.edu.au/lss/registration>.

GRANTS

The Organising Committee of the ANU Logic Summer School is pleased to announce a limited number of grants that might cover, in part, the registration fee, accommodation costs, travel expenses. The deadline for applications is 18 September 2026. For more details on conditions and application, please consult our web-page<http://comp.anu.edu.au/lss/grants>.

ORGANISATION

The logic summer school is organised by Ranald Clouston, Peter Höfner, and Michael Norrish. Please direct all enquiries to lss.comp@anu.edu.au<mailto:lss.comp@anu.edu.au>.
