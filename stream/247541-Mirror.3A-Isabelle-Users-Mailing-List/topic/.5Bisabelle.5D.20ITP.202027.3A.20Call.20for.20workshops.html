 18th International Conference on Interactive Theorem Proving - ITP'27
 University of Strathclyde, Glasgow, Scotland, 2-7 August 2027
https://itp-conference-2027.github.io/
 Call for Workshop and Tutorial Proposals
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The ITP conference organisers are soliciting proposals for affiliated
workshops and tutorials. Members of the community are invited to
submit proposals on any topic related to interactive theorem proving.

Workshops typically feature presentations of ongoing research that is
not ready yet for formal publication, and tutorials may e.g. present
extended demos of particular software.

The ITP organisers encourage a broad interpretation of the above
formats and welcome proposals for any kind of event that benefits the
ITP community. The workshops and tutorials will take place on the
2nd and 7th of August.

Workshop/tutorial proposals should contain the following data:
 - Name of the workshop/tutorial.
 - Brief description of the workshop/tutorial, including workshop topics.
 - Previous instances of the event (if any).
 - Contact information and affiliation of the workshop/tutorial organisers.
 - An estimate of the audience size.
 - Proposed format of the workshop/tutorial (e.g. regular talks, tool
demos, poster presentations, etc.).
 - Duration of the workshop/tutorial (e.g. full-day or half-day) and
the preferred day. We cannot guarantee that preferences can be followed.
 - Special technical or AV needs (if any).

All proposals should be submitted by e-mail to:
CIS_itp2027@groups.strath.ac.uk

The deadline for submitting a proposal is Thursday 14th January 2027.
Earlier submissions are encouraged, and notifications of acceptance
will be issued on a rolling basis.

-- The local organisers
Guillaume Allais, Jan de Muijnck-Hughes, and Fredrik Nordvall Forsberg
