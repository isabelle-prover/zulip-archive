lemma rank_uminus [simp]:
shows "ρ[- f] = ρ[f]"
by (metis nle_le pointwise_op_def rank_pointwise_op uminus_FR_def verit_minus_simplify(4))

"verit_minus_simplify(4)" is no longer known

lemma leq_FR_antisymmetric:
assumes "leq_FR f g ∧ leq_FR g f"
shows "f = g"
unfolding leq_FR_def
by (meson antisym_conv1 assms eq_iff_eq_val leq_FR_def order_less_irrefl verit_la_disequality)

"verit_la_disequality" is no longer known

lemma leq_FR_pointwise_op:
assumes "⋀x y. x ≤ y ⟹ h x ≤ h y"
and "leq_FR f g"
shows "leq_FR (pointwise_op h f) (pointwise_op h g)"
using assms
by (metis leq_FR_def val_pointwise_op verit_comp_simplify1(2))

"verit_comp_simplify1(2)" is no longer known

Subject: [isabelle] Isabelle2026-RC3 missing "verit" facts that worked in  Isabelle 2026-Rc0

Hi Gene,

All the facts have been renamed from verit_<name> to alethe_<name>. The veriT solver's output format has been refined and is now called Alethe and also used by cvc5.

To avoid issues like this one in the future, the lemmas have now been marked as [no_atp], which is why Sledgehammer won't find them.

Best,
Hanna Lachnitt
