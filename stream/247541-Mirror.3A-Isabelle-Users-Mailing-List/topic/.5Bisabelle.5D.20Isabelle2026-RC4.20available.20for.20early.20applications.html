There is been no further activity on this thread, so I am assuming that
everything is fine, according to the social contract behind Isabelle releases
(since 2008):

* The overall quality of the system becomes gradually better.
* Serious regressions that were recently introduced have been addressed.

Consequently, I will produce the final and unchangeable Isabelle2026 release
in approx. 12h, but it will take 1-2 days afterwards to get everything
synchronized and published.

Unless, someone presents new problems that justify to postpone the final
release, e.g. for 1 week.

Reminder: Isabelle has a linear release model, without "hotfixes" nor
"patches". Instead there will be eventually another regular release, at a
distance of 8-10 months.

Makarius
