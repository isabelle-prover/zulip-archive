Subject: [isabelle] Isabelle2026: repeated Poly/ML crashes during minor GC in x86_64_32 mode

Dear Isabelle users,

I have encountered repeated segmentation faults in the Poly/ML component
bundled with Isabelle2026 during interactive theory processing in
Isabelle/jEdit.

Analysis of a core dump using GDB suggests an unsigned integer underflow in
OSMemInRegion::AllocateDataArea during minor GC.

I have reported the detailed findings, including the backtrace, register
values, source-level analysis and a possible fix, to the Poly/ML issue
tracker:

https://github.com/polyml/polyml/issues/347

Since this issue occurs with the official Isabelle2026 distribution, I
thought it would also be worth bringing to the attention of the Isabelle
community.

Best regards,
Taiga Sagi
