Formalization of Lexicographic Breadth-First Search

Thomas Holme Surlykke <https://isa-afp.org/authors/surlykke/> and
Dmitriy Traytel <https://isa-afp.org/authors/traytel/> :e-mail:
<mailto:traytel@di.ku.dk>

November 18, 2026

Abstract

Despite widespread use of the lexicographic breadth-first search graph
ordering algorithm, the literature lacks a complete proof characterizing
its output. We verify that the standard characterization provides a
sound and complete specification. Our approach introduces a binary
reformulation of the label function, exposing intermediate states and
enabling proof through monadic refinement.
