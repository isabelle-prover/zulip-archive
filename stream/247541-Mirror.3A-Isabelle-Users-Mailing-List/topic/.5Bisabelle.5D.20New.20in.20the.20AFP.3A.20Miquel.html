Miquel's Theorem in Isabelle/HOL
Arthur Freitas Ramos, David Barros Hulak and Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz

We formalize Miquel's theorem, also known as the pivot theorem, in Isabelle/HOL.
Let be a triangle in the Euclidean plane and let ... be points on the side lines
... respectively. Then the circumcircles of the three triangles ... pass through
a common point, the Miquel point of the configuration.

The development uses complex coordinates.

https://isa-afp.org/entries/Miquel.html
