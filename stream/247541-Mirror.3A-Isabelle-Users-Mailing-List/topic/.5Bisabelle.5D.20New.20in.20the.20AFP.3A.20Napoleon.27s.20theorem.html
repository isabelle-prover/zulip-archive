I'm happy to announce another contribution by Arthur Freitas Ramos, David Barros Hulak and Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz.

Napoleon's Theorem

Napoleon's theorem states that, for a non-degenerate triangle, the centres of the equilateral triangles constructed externally on its three sides form an equilateral triangle. We formalize this construction and conclusion using complex coordinates, including the exterior-side condition and both orientations of the 60-degree rotation.

https://isa-afp.org/entries/Napoleon.html
