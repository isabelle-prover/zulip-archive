Runtime Extensions for BMSSP
Arthur Freitas Ramos, David Barros Hulak and Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz

This entry extends the AFP formalization Correctness and Complexity of BMSSP in Isabelle/HOL, which formalizes the bounded multi-source shortest path algorithm of Duan, Mao, Mao, Shu, and Yin. The extension adds a charged direct-insert relation that records both the range charged by the recurrence and the semantic output returned by each recursive child. It proves that least costs are realized by verified-correct runs, exhibits a closed branching instance, and records a machine-checked totality obstruction for the older diagonal exact-concrete path-family relation. Its main theorem, path_nonvac_runtime_bigo_card_V_unconditional, proves without run-existence, cost-bound, or closed-bound assumptions that the least charged cost on the growing unit-weight path family is O(|V_n| log^{2/3} |V_n|)\) when the internal schedule is evaluated at n^2. This is a cost-relation theorem, not a machine-step bound for the SML executable from the base entry.
