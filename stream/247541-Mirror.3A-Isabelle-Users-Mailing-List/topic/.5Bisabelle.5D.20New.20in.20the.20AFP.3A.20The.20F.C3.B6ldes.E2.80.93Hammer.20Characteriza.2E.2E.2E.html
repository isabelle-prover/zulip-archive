Dear all,

I’d like to announce a new AFP entry:

The Földes–Hammer Characterization of Split Graphs
by Arthur Freitas Ramos, David Barros Hulak and Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz

This entry formalizes the classical Földes–Hammer theorem characterizing finite split graphs
as exactly the graphs with no induced copy of 2 K_2, C_4, or C_5.
It builds on the AFP session Undirected_Graph_Theory.

https://isa-afp.org/entries/Foldes_Hammer.html
