Subject: [isabelle] New in the AFP: The Isoperimetric Inequality for Rectifiable Simple Closed Curves

The Isoperimetric Inequality for Rectifiable Simple Closed Curves
Lawrence C. Paulson

This entry formalises the planar isoperimetric inequality for rectifiable simple
closed curves. If a curve has length L, the area of its inside is at most L^2/(4
pi); equality implies that its image is a circle. The development proves a
Wirtinger inequality, a special area formula for convex curves, and a
convexification argument. It builds on the Fourier AFP entry and analysis
material incorporated into Isabelle2026.

https://isa-afp.org/entries/Isoperimetric.html
