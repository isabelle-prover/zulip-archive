August 11, 2026

Abstract

We formalize a trace-based Rely-Guarantee semantics based on the work of
Xu et al. The foundation includes an abstract Sequential/While/Parallel
programming language equipped with operational semantics designed for
concurrent reasoning. Upon this, we build a reasoning infrastructure
that includes Rely-Guarantee inversion rules, alongside abstract
definitions and standard soundness proofs for both unary and binary
settings.
