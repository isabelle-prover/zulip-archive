Subject: [isabelle] PhD Position in Resilient Machine Learning and Formal Methods (TU Eindhoven, Netherlands)

The newly established "Center for Safe AI" at Eindhoven University of Technology (Netherlands) is looking for a PhD candidate to bring rigorous formal methods to resilient machine-learning systems. The candidate will join a unique interdisciplinary project at the interface of automated reasoning, probabilistic verification, and machine learning. Over this four-year PhD position, the goal is to develop machine-learning algorithms that are not only robust but also resilient by recovering and learning from failures at runtime. To ensure trustworthiness, these algorithms will be developed with a focus on formal guarantees on resilience.

A prerequisite is a very good Master's degree in computer science, mathematics, or a related field, and a strong background in theory (formal methods, theoretical computer science, foundations of machine learning).

Application deadline: 20 August 2026 (position stays open until filled)

More information and details at
https://www.tue.nl/en/working-at-tue/vacancy-overview/phd-in-resilient-machine-learning-and-formal-methods

We look forward to receiving your applications. Please share the link with colleagues and interested candidates that are passionate about formal methods and artificial intelligence.
