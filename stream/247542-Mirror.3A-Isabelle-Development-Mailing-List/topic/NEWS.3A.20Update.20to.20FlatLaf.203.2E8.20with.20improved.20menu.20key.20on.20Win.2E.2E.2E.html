  • The FlatLaf look-and-feel (version 3.8) now handles F10 on Windows
    more natively, which is important for the NVDA Screen Reader.

This refers to Isabelle/64e27bb34f39.

https://github.com/JFormDesigner/FlatLaf/releases/tag/3.8

mathematician from Linz, see also
https://github.com/JFormDesigner/FlatLaf/issues/1093
